      How old is the light from the Sun?

      About 8 minutes and 20 seconds. You always see the Sun as it was a moment ago.

      What is odd about Venus?

      One Venus day (243 Earth days) is longer than its year (225 days), and it spins backwards.

      How heavy is a neutron star?

      A teaspoon of its matter would weigh about a billion tonnes.

      Stars or sand?

      Estimates put the stars in the observable universe above the number of sand grains on every beach on Earth.
